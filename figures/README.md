## Contents

- **`correlation_heatmap.png`** - Pearson correlation matrixs for a small subset of stocks spanning consumer staples (KO, PEP, MDLZ), tech (AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL), and energy (XOM, CVX), so all correlation is visable.

- **`eigenvalue-spectrum.png`** - Full eigenvalue spectrum of the S&P 500 correlation matrix (asinh y-scale), plotted gainst the Marchehnko-Pastur upper and lower bounds. Eiegnvalues inside the shaded bounds are consistent with pure noise. Eigenvalues above the upper bound reprsent statistically signficant correlation.
